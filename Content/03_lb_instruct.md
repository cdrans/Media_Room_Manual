# How to Use The Lightboard Station

```{warning} Warning
This sub-chapter is under construction. The content below is currently in draft form and will be refined and enriched with images in the coming weeks.
```

This chapter is a complete walkthrough of the Lightboard station, from turning on the system to shutting everything down at the end of your session. For guidance on planning your presentation slides, writing technique, and what to wear, see the Tips & Tricks chapter.

# Before You Start
Make sure you have the following with you when you arrive:
- [ ] A USB stick with your PowerPoint presentation (and slide notes, if you plan to use the autocue). The studio computer is not connected to the internet, so bring everything you need with you.
- [ ] Enough free space on the USB stick to also save your recording (recommended to have at least 2 GB for 1 hour of recording time).
- [ ] Any physical materials you want to show under the document camera.

```{note} Note
Lightboard markers are already provided in the studio, you don't need to bring your own. If you don't have a presentation at all, that's fine too: a blank template is available on the desktop so you can write directly on the lightboard.
```

# Step-by-step Guidance
## Step 1: Starting the System
Turn on the WebClip2Go recording system by pressing and holding the main power button.

:::{figure} ./figures/Turn_on_the_system.gif
:name: fig-webclip2go_powerup
:alt: Animation of pressing the powerup sequence button for the lightboard recording system
:width: 70%
:::

Pressing and holding the main power button will initiate the main startup procedure for the WebClip2Go system. A voice recording will indicate that this procedure has started and will remind you of the duration of this procedure (approximately 3 min). During this startup procedure, recording lights, microphones, and cameras will be activated and the computer (herby refered to as the *input-computer*) at the end of the table in the studio will startup. It is through this input-computer that users will interact with the system.

```{note} Note
The input-computer functions on a generic profile and thus does not require you to login with your NetID and password. As a consequence of utilizing a generic profile, the computer is isolated from the TUDelft network for security reasons and does not have internet access. This means all files (ie: ppt presentations, finished recordings) can only be uploaded or downloaded from the computer using a USB storage device.
```

## Step 2: Setting Up the Lightboard Station
The same camera and computer system is used for the Lightboard and the Greenscreen recording stations. Thus, when you enter the media room, you might have to reconfigure the space for a Lightboard session. That can be done through the following steps:

1. Move the Lightboard into place such that the wheels of the lightboard align with the yellow tape marks on the floor and the side of the lightboard with the below graphic faces the window. 

:::{figure} ./figures/lb_lightboard_placement_instructions.png
:name: fig-lightboard_placement_instructions
:alt: Instructional graphic showing how to line up the lightboard.
:width: 70%
:::

:::{figure} ./figures/Yellow_Marks.gif
:name: fig-Yellow_Marks
:alt: Animated image showing how the lightboard should align with the yellow marks on the floor.
:width: 60%
:::

2. Test the lightboard markers. If there are no markers on the lightboard itself, they can be accessed from the grey storage cabinet behind the camera labeled *Lightboard Supplies*. You can also find additional supplies such as cleaning clothes in this cabinet.

3. Turn on the Lightboard screen and presenter lights using the lightswitches on the side of the lightboard. If the lights do not turn on, please ensure the power cord next to the switches is connected to an electrical socket.

:::{figure} ./figures/lb_lightboard_lighting_instructions.png
:name: fig-lightboard_lighting_instructions
:alt: Instructional graphic showing how to turn on the lightboard lights.
:width: 70%
:::

4. If necessary, clean the board. To clean the board, use the dry cloth from the supplies bag. It works well once the surface is dry. Don't wet the cloth or use a spray bottle for routine cleaning. It causes the marker residue to smear across the glass instead of lifting off. If a deep clean is needed, use spray cleaner with paper towel instead of the cloth.

## Step 3: Starting a New Recording Session
Start a new recoding session by opening the WebClip2Go application and setting up the specifics of your recording session. This is broken down further in the sub-steps below:

1. Using the input-computer, click on the *Autonomous WebClip2Go Controller* shortcut to open the WebClip2Go software.

:::{figure} ./figures/Webclip2go_openen.gif
:name: fig-webclip2go_program_startup
:alt: Animated image showing the shortcut to click on to start the program.
:width: 60%
:::

2. Start a new session and choose the *Lightboard format*. WebClip2Go offers four formats. The two labelled “Lightboard” are the ones you want; the other two are for the Green Screen station. “Branded” includes the TU Delft intro animation, “unbranded” skips it.

:::{figure} ./figures/Lightboard_choose_format.gif
:name: fig-lightboard_choose_format
:alt: Animated image showing the dropdown selection menu to choose the WebClip2Go recording format.
:width: 60%
:::

3. Select your PowerPoint file (field highlighted with yellow arrow below). The system allows you to use a Powerpoint slide deck to overlay images and text on top of your session as demonstrated in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJUuI49Noio). For this overlay to work, the background colour of your sides must be set to (RGB 0,255,0) for proper background removal. Upload your presentation from your storage device **OR** use the blank Lightboard PowerPoint template located on the desktop of the input-computer. Choose where the recording should be saved when prompted.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_powerpoint_file.png
:name: fig-lbi_powerpoint_file
:alt: Image of WebClip2Go screen with a yellow arrow highlighting the field to click and navigate to the desired PowerPoint file to be used in the Lightboard session
:width: 70%
:::

4. Choose the layout for the Teleprompter Screen (visible to you while recording). For recording a Lightboard session, it is recommended that you choose the option *Preview in main part, cue text at right*. This will ensure that the largest window in the teleprompter (main part) shows a live view of what the camera is recording. The Cue Text window on the right will show the notes section of the Powerpoint slide, allowing you to have some notes visible while looking at the camera.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_prompter_layout.png
:name: fig-lbi_prompter_layout
:alt: Image of WebClip2Go screen with a yellow arrow highlighting the drop-down menu to specify the layout of the autocue preview.
:width: 60%
:::

5. Turn on speech recognition if required. This option is generally not required for a lightboard session as you are not typically reading word-for-word of an autocue when recording such a video. However, it is an option, so if you need the autocue to scroll through your script while you read it, you can turn this option on.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_speech_recognition.png
:name: fig-lbi_speech_recognition
:alt: Image of WebClip2Go screen showing speech recognition for autocue controls
:width: 60%
:::

6. After clicking next to complete the previous screen, simply click through the participant details screen (shown below) by pressing "Finished" as this screen is not relevant for a Lightboard session.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_skip.png
:name: fig-lbi_skip
:alt: Image of WebClip2Go screen with speaker detail fields. These fields are not used in the Lightboard and can be skipped.
:width: 80%
:::

7. On the PowerPoint Slidedeck Preview Screen, set the Layout of the second slide of the presentation to *Lightboard* as shown below. This ensures that when you click through your presentation, it will ensure that the Lightboard layout is applied to your entire presentation.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_powerpoint_slide_layout.png

## Step 4: Preparing the Room and Testing the Setup

There can be a number of settings that may have inadvertently been setup incorrectly in the previous steps, making it important to complete the final room preparations and perform a test recording to ensure lighting, audio, and other settings are correct before you embark on your first recording.

**Final Room Preparations:** 
- Ensure the slider on the Media Room door is set from "Vrij" to "Niet Storen" to ensure someone does not enter the room while you are recording.

:::{figure} ./figures/Do_not_disturb.gif
:name: fig-Do_not_disturb
:alt: Animation of the slider on the media room door where users can indicate that a recording session is taking place and that they should not enter the room.
:width: 60%
:::

- Turn off the main room florecent lights (lightswitch near the door) and ensure all of the window blinds are closed. This should be done after you turn on the WebClip2Go system and the Lightboard lights as otherwise it will be very dark in the room. 

- Adjust the camera to eye level using the arrow controls on the desk. This can also be done/tweaked during the test recording.

:::{figure} ./figures/lbi_height_adjust.png
:name: fig-lbi_height_adjust
:alt: Height adjustment buttons for adjusting the height of the camera setup.
:width: 70%
:::

**Making a Test Recording:**
Before recording the full presentation, make a short test recording and check the following:
- The camera is at the right height. You shouldn't see the edges of the lightboard in frame.
- Your writing is clearly visible and you're not blocking it.
- The lighting mode is correct.
- The board is completely clean, with no leftover marker stains.
- The audio was recorded. Check that your mic picked up sound.

Once you complete the test recording, you can play it on the input-computer from the save location you specified during setup.

## Step 5: Record
Recording of your lightboard session is controlled with a clicker and by the powerpoint slide template. Every click of the clicker will progress through the PowerPoint slide deck and once the end of the presentation is reached, the recording will be stopped.

- On the clicker, press the black button once to start recording. This begins the countdown and, if selected, the intro animation.

:::{figure} ./figures/Black_Button.gif
:name: fig-Black_Button
:alt: Animated image showing the clicker and the black button used to control the recording.
:width: 30%
:::

```{warning} Red Button = Cancel Recording!
Never press the red button on the clicker during recording. It stops the whole recording. Your recording is only saved automatically after the final slide, so pressing red before that point will result in your entire recording being deleted!
```

- Use the same black button to move to the next slide while presenting.

```{note} When Using the Intro Slide Format...
If you selected the format with the intro slide, wait until it has finished before you start speaking. The camera only switches on once you click past it. Once you reach the end of your presentation, an outro video plays automatically.
```

- Finish your recording. Present through to the last slide. The recording stops and saves automatically once you reach it.

```{note} Cleaning the Board
Erasing the entire board while recording is difficult, so don't try to do it live. If you need to clear it, stop the recording, clean the board, and continue with a second, separate recording. Small erases along the way, such as cleaning up a single word or line, are fine to do during recording.
```

## Step 6: Finishing Up
Once your recording is complete, do the following:

1. Review your recording. Quickly check the sound, the slide timing, and that you're clearly visible. If something's wrong, you can start a new session and record again.

2. Save the video to your own USB storage device. The input-computer hard drive will be periodically be cleaned to preserve space, thus it is important that you transfer the recordings to your own storage device before leaving. Unfortunately, the input-computer is not connected to the internet, so you can only transfer files using an external usb storage device.

```{note} Forgot a USB Storage Device?
If you forgot your own USB stick, there is a spare one kept in the supplies bag that can be used for transferring your file in a pinch. Please delete your files from it afterwards. There is currently no lock on the supplies storage, so don't leave anything on it that you wouldn't want others to see.
```

3. Record again if needed. Use “Edit session” to upload a new PowerPoint, change the layout, or start another recording.

4.	Shut down. Remove your USB stick and delete your video from the computer, including emptying the recycle bin. Put all equipment back in its place, move the lightboard back to its original position, and make sure you've used the correct markers.

5. Before leaving, turn of the WebClip2Go system by pressing and holding the system power button (see step 1). 

6. Switch the door label back to "Vrij" on your way out of the room.

## Troubleshooting
- No sound in your recording: this is a technical issue, call down to the desk and ask for someone from the New Media Centre.
- No markers available: ask for new ones at the Teaching Lab desk.
- Recording starts too early: wait until the intro animation has finished before you begin talking.
- Text cut off in the autocue: add extra empty lines after your title slide so the first spoken lines appear correctly.
- Lightboard writing not visible: check that the text light is switched on and test your markers before recording.
- Anything else: ask at the front desk downstairs.
