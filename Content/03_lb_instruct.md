This chapter is a complete walkthrough of the Lightboard station, from turning on the system to shutting everything down at the end of your session. For guidance on planning your presentation slides, writing technique, and what to wear, see the Tips & Tricks chapter.

# Before You Start
Make sure you have the following with you when you arrive:
- [ ] A USB stick with your PowerPoint presentation (and slide notes, if you plan to use the autocue). The studio computer is not connected to the internet, so bring everything you need with you.
- [ ] Enough free space on the USB stick to also save your recording (recommended to have at least 2 GB for 1 hour of recording time).
- [ ] Any physical materials you want to show under the document camera.

```{note} Note
Lightboard markers are already provided in the studio, you don't need to bring your own. If you don't have a presentation at all, that's fine too: a blank template is available on the desktop so you can write directly on the lightboard.
```

# Step-by-step Guidance
## Step 1: Starting the System
Turn on the WebClip2Go recording system by pressing and holding the main power button.

:::{figure} ./figures/Turn_on_the_system.gif
:name: fig-webclip2go_powerup
:alt: Animation of pressing the powerup sequence button for the lightboard recording system
:width: 70%
:::

Pressing and holding the main power button will initiate the main startup procedure for the WebClip2Go system. A voice recording will indicate that this procedure has started and will remind you of the duration of this procedure (approximately 3 min). During this startup procedure, recording lights, microphones, and cameras will be activated and the computer (herby refered to as the *input-computer*) at the end of the table in the studio will startup. It is through this input-computer that users will interact with the system.

```{note} Note
The input-computer functions on a generic profile and thus does not require you to login with your NetID and password. As a consequence of utilizing a generic profile, the computer is isolated from the TUDelft network for security reasons and does not have internet access. This means all files (ie: ppt presentations, finished recordings) can only be uploaded or downloaded from the computer using a USB storage device.
```
